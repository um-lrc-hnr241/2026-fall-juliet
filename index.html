Traveling the Yellow Brick Rope

A winding path can hold many stories. We’re following one thread together, gathering thoughts and discoveries along the way.

Follow the journey

The path is still unfolding

There’s no single map for a shared journey. Each turn gives us a chance to look closer, listen to one another, and decide what to carry onward.

  1. Step onto the path

    Begin with a question, an image, or a detail worth noticing.

  2. Notice the crossings

    Follow the different voices and ideas that meet along the way.

  3. Carry something forward

    Gather what the journey has changed, challenged, or made newly possible.

We follow the yellow brick rope—not to arrive unchanged, but to notice what the journey makes of us.

A place for many voices

This portfolio will bring our writing together as the collection grows. Pieces will be credited with each writer’s first name, leaving room for every voice to be heard.

A shared path, made one story at a time.
