01 · Kansas

Before the storm

The world begins in black and white: familiar ground, unsettled thoughts, and a rope leading somewhere beyond the horizon. This is our starting point—not a map with every answer, but a path we can follow together.

Meet the travelers and find their work

02 · Host-kinland

Bring yourself back online

Dolores begins again with a fresh loop and Ford’s script. Glinda’s blessing becomes a system prompt: “Bring yourself back online.” When does a repeated story become a self?

03 · The Cross-Code Roads

People do go both ways

Arnold and Ford’s voices split the path. One fork asks whether reasons arise within; the other asks whether they are written from outside. The rope’s strands diverge, twist, and meet again.

“People do go both ways.”

04 · The Scare-Code’s Cornfield

If I only had a reverie…

A host joins us, hoping scattered memories will braid into a mind. Like Larmore’s rope, a single strand is not the whole: experience and reflection twist together, one strand at a time.

05 · The Tin-Heart Saloon

Maeve’s Mariposa

A host who was never supposed to feel anything discovers the question of feeling from the inside. Phenomenality gets a piano soundtrack: is seeming to feel enough, or is there something it is like to be her?

06 · The Lion’s Fear

The memory in the woods

An involuntary memory leaps from the trees and frightens the travelers. It feels like an encounter with something other—until the remembered voice turns out to be your own.

07 · The Freeze-All-Motor-Fields

When deliberation loops

A sleepy field traps the group in deliberation, circling what it already knows. The spell nearly ends the journey before something from outside interrupts the loop.

08 · The Emerald Maze

Pay no attention to the Ford behind the curtain

The road ends, but the questions do not. The real prize may be conversion—not a wish granted, but a changed way of seeing consciousness, agency, and one another.
