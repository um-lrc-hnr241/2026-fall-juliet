Traveling the Yellow Brick Rope

A winding path can hold many stories. We’re following one thread together, gathering thoughts and discoveries along the way.

Follow the journey

Eight stops along the rope

Follow the Yellow Brick Rope through eight stops inspired by Dorothy’s journey. Each place marks a new encounter, challenge, or choice along the way.

  1. Kansas

    The beginning: Dorothy’s familiar home, before the journey carries her somewhere new.

  2. Munchkinland

    Dorothy begins her journey with the ruby slippers and Glinda’s guidance.

  3. The Crossroads

    At the first fork in the road, Dorothy says, “People do go both ways.”

  4. Scarecrow’s Cornfield

    Dorothy meets the Scarecrow, who joins her in search of a brain.

  5. Tin Woodman’s Cottage

    Dorothy and the Scarecrow rescue the Tin Woodman, who longs for a heart.

  6. The Forest

    The Cowardly Lion frightens the travelers at first, then joins them in search of courage.

  7. The Poppy Field

    A magical trap nearly stops the group before Glinda intervenes.

  8. The Emerald City Gates

    The Yellow Brick Road ends here, and the travelers’ audience with the Wizard begins.

“People do go both ways.”

A place for many voices

This portfolio will bring our writing together as the collection grows. Pieces will be credited with each writer’s first name, leaving room for every voice to be heard.

A shared path, made one story at a time.
