Traveling the Yellow Brick Rope

I set out along the Yellow Brick Rope, meeting new companions and gathering discoveries at every turn. My journey unfolds through eight builds, one for each stop along the road.

Follow the journey

My eight builds along the rope

Travel with me through eight builds inspired by Dorothy’s journey. Each stop is its own subsection: a new encounter, challenge, or choice along my way.

  1. Build 1: Kansas

    I begin in Kansas, where home feels familiar before the journey carries me somewhere new.

  2. Build 2: Munchkinland

    I arrive with the ruby slippers and Glinda’s guidance, ready to follow the road.

  3. Build 3: The Crossroads

    I reach my first fork in the road and remind myself, “People do go both ways.”

  4. Build 4: Scarecrow’s Cornfield

    I meet the Scarecrow in his cornfield, and he joins me in search of a brain.

  5. Build 5: Tin Woodman’s Cottage

    The Scarecrow and I rescue the Tin Woodman, who joins us hoping to find a heart.

  6. Build 6: The Forest

    In the forest, the Cowardly Lion frightens us at first, then joins our search for courage.

  7. Build 7: The Poppy Field

    A magical trap nearly stops our journey in the poppy field, until Glinda intervenes.

  8. Build 8: The Emerald City Gates

    I reach the end of the Yellow Brick Road, where our audience with the Wizard begins.

“People do go both ways.”

A place for many voices

This portfolio will bring our writing together as the collection grows. Pieces will be credited with each writer’s first name, leaving room for every voice to be heard.

A shared path, made one story at a time.
